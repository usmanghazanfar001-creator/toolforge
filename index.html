Frequently asked questions

Are ToolForge AI tools really free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use, with no account, trial or hidden limit. The site is supported by advertising.

Is my data private?

Most tools run entirely in your browser, so what you type is not sent to us. The AI tools are the exception: their text is sent to our server and a third-party AI provider to generate a result, and is not stored by ToolForge. The currency converter fetches live rates but does not store your amounts.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Open a tool and use it immediately.

Do the tools work on phones and tablets?

Yes. The site is responsive and every tool works on modern mobile browsers, in light or dark mode.

How accurate are the calculators?

They use standard, published formulas and show the working where it helps. Results are estimates for learning and planning, so confirm anything important, such as loan terms or official grades, with the relevant institution.

How do the AI tools work?

You give a short input, the tool sends it to an AI model with task-specific instructions, and you receive the result. Inputs are capped at 6,000 characters, and AI output can contain mistakes, so always review it.
