Contact ToolForge AI

We would love to hear from you. Whether you found a bug, spotted an error or have an idea for a new tool, get in touch.

Email

Write to us at hello@aitoolforge.vercel.app. We aim to reply within three to five working days.

What to write to us about

  • Bug reports: tell us the tool name, what you entered, what you expected and what happened. Your browser and device help too.
  • Incorrect results or content errors: include the inputs and the answer you believe is correct. We review corrections promptly and update the page.
  • Tool suggestions: describe the problem you are trying to solve and we will consider it for the roadmap.
  • Privacy requests: questions about how data is handled are covered in our Privacy Policy.
  • Partnerships and press: send a short note about your idea.

Before you write

Each tool page has a FAQ and a how-to guide, and our blog covers common questions in more depth. We cannot give personal medical, legal, financial or academic advice. Please do not send passwords, tokens or other confidential information by email.
