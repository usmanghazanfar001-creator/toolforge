About ToolForge AI

ToolForge AI is a free collection of online tools for developers, students and everyday work. Each tool is built to do one job well, load fast and respect your privacy.

Why ToolForge exists

Many utility websites are slow, buried in pop-ups, ask you to sign up for a simple calculation or quietly upload whatever you paste into them. We wanted a cleaner alternative: open a page, get the answer, and leave. Most of our tools run entirely in your browser, which means your text, numbers and images never have to leave your device.

What you will find here

  • Developer tools such as the JSON formatter, Base64 and URL encoders, JWT decoder, regex tester, hash generator and UUID generator.
  • Student tools including CGPA, GCD, statistics, fraction, matrix and quadratic equation calculators that show their working, so you learn the method as well as the answer.
  • Everyday calculators and converters for percentages, loans, interest, age, dates, units, currencies and time zones.
  • Text, image and generator tools like the word counter, text cleaner, image compressor and resizer, QR code and password generators.
  • AI writing helpers for prompts, summaries, rewrites, captions, emails and blog outlines.

How we handle your data

Non-AI tools process everything locally in your browser. The AI tools are different: the text you submit is sent to our server and on to a third-party AI provider to generate a response. It is limited to 6,000 characters per request and is not stored by ToolForge. The currency converter fetches live exchange rates from a public service. Our Privacy Policy explains this in full.

How we keep content accurate

Calculators use standard, published formulas and, where useful, display the working so you can check it. Tool guides and blog posts are written and reviewed by the ToolForge AI editorial team and updated when we find an error or a better explanation. Results are informational: for decisions about health, money or grades, confirm with a qualified professional or your institution. See our Disclaimer.

How the site is funded

ToolForge AI is free to use and supported by advertising from Google AdSense. Ads never change tool results, and we do not sell your data.

Who is behind it

ToolForge AI is an independent project built and maintained by NOVIQO. We read every message and use your feedback to decide which tools to improve or build next.
