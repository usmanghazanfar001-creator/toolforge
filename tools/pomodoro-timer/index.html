About the Pomodoro Study Timer

The Pomodoro Technique splits work into short focused sprints, traditionally 25 minutes, separated by 5-minute breaks, with a longer break after four rounds. The time limit keeps you from drifting and the breaks protect your concentration. This timer runs in your browser with focus, short break and long break modes, so you can start a study session in one click without installing anything.

When to use it

  • Studying for exams in structured, distraction-free blocks.
  • Writing, coding or design work that needs sustained focus.
  • Breaking an overwhelming task into small, manageable sessions.
💡 Pro tip

Use the break to stand up, stretch or drink water rather than checking your phone. Screens during breaks make it harder to refocus.

Frequently asked questions

How long is a Pomodoro?

The classic length is 25 minutes of focus followed by a 5-minute break. After four cycles take a longer break of 15 to 30 minutes.

Can I change the session length?

Yes. Adjust focus and break durations to suit your task and concentration span.

Does the timer keep running if I switch tabs?

Keep the tab open for reliable timing, because browsers can throttle background tabs.
