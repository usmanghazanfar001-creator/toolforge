About the Text Summarizer

Long articles, reports and lecture notes take time to digest. Paste your text, choose a short, medium or long summary, and this AI tool condenses it while keeping key facts, names and numbers. Your text is sent to our server and an AI provider for processing, up to 6,000 characters at a time, and is not stored by ToolForge. Always compare the summary against the source for anything important.

When to use it

  • Getting the gist of a long article before deciding to read it.
  • Condensing study notes into revision points.
  • Summarizing meeting notes or reports for a quick update.
💡 Pro tip

For long documents, summarize section by section and then summarize the summaries. This keeps more detail than one huge pass.

Frequently asked questions

Is my text private?

It is sent to our server and an AI provider to generate the summary. We do not store it, but avoid pasting confidential or sensitive material.

How long can the input be?

Up to 6,000 characters per request. Split longer documents into sections.

Can the summary contain mistakes?

AI can occasionally misread or omit details. Verify important facts against the original text.
