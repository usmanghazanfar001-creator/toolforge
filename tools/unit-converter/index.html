About the Unit Converter

Recipes, travel, schoolwork and engineering all mix metric and imperial units. This converter handles the most common categories, length, weight, area and temperature, and updates the answer as you type. Conversions use exact standard factors, so you can trust the result for homework and everyday use alike, without opening a spreadsheet or searching for a formula.

When to use it

  • Converting kilometers to miles or feet to meters for travel and fitness.
  • Switching between kilograms and pounds for shipping or recipes.
  • Translating Celsius to Fahrenheit for weather and cooking temperatures.
💡 Pro tip

To convert Celsius to Fahrenheit, multiply by 9/5 and add 32. For a quick mental estimate, double the Celsius value and add 30.

Frequently asked questions

How many feet are in a meter?

One meter is about 3.2808 feet, and one foot is exactly 0.3048 meters.

Why is temperature conversion different from other units?

Celsius and Fahrenheit have different zero points, so conversion needs both multiplication and an offset, not just a factor.

How accurate are the results?

They use standard defined conversion factors and display up to six decimal places.
