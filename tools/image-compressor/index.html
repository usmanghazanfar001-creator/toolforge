About the Image Compressor

Large images slow down websites, bloat emails and exceed upload limits. This compressor re-encodes your image in the browser with an adjustable quality setting and shows the before and after file size so you can find the best balance. Because the work happens on your device, your photos are not uploaded to any server, which keeps private pictures private.

When to use it

  • Shrinking photos to meet a website or form upload limit.
  • Speeding up a blog or store by optimizing hero images.
  • Making images small enough to send by email or chat.
💡 Pro tip

For photos, a quality of 70 to 80 percent usually cuts the file size dramatically with almost no visible difference. Convert to WebP for even smaller files.

Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Compression runs entirely in your browser, and the image never leaves your device.

Which format compresses best?

WebP generally gives the smallest files for photos at similar quality, JPG is widely compatible, and PNG suits graphics with sharp edges or transparency.

Will compression reduce quality?

Lossy compression removes some detail. Choose a higher quality value if you see artifacts.
