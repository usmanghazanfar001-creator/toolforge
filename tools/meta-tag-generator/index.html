About the Meta Tag Generator

Meta tags tell search engines and social networks how to display your page: the title and description in search results and the image and text when a link is shared. Fill in your page details and this generator produces clean HTML for the title, description, canonical, Open Graph and Twitter tags, ready to paste into your head section. It also shows length hints so your snippets are not cut off.

When to use it

  • Adding SEO tags to a new landing page or blog post.
  • Making links look good when shared on social media.
  • Checking that your title and description fit within display limits.
💡 Pro tip

Write the meta description for humans. A clear benefit-focused sentence under about 155 characters earns more clicks than a keyword list.

Frequently asked questions

How long should a title tag be?

Aim for about 50 to 60 characters so it is not truncated in search results.

What are Open Graph tags?

They are meta tags, started by Facebook, that control the title, description and image shown when your page is shared on social platforms.

Do meta descriptions affect rankings?

Not directly, but a good description raises click-through rate, which helps your traffic.
