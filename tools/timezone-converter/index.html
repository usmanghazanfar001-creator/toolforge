About the Time Zone Converter

Scheduling a call across continents means translating one clock into another while accounting for daylight saving time. Choose a date, time and source zone and this converter shows the equivalent time in other locations using the browser's built-in time zone database, so daylight saving shifts are handled correctly for the chosen date.

When to use it

  • Finding a meeting time that suits teammates in different countries.
  • Checking what time a live event starts in your own zone.
  • Planning calls to family or clients abroad.
💡 Pro tip

Always set the exact date. Daylight saving starts and ends on different dates in different countries, so the gap between two cities changes during the year.

Frequently asked questions

Does it handle daylight saving time?

Yes. It uses the IANA time zone database built into your browser, which includes daylight saving rules.

Why does the difference between two cities change?

Countries start and end daylight saving on different dates, and some do not use it at all.

What is UTC?

Coordinated Universal Time is the global reference that all time zones are defined against.
