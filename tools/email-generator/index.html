About the AI Email Generator

Staring at a blank email wastes time. Describe the purpose, such as asking for an extension or following up on an invoice, pick a tone, and this AI tool drafts a subject line and message you can edit and send. Your description is sent to our server and an AI provider to generate the draft, and it is not stored by ToolForge.

When to use it

  • Writing a polite follow-up or request quickly.
  • Drafting a professional message when you are unsure of the right wording.
  • Creating a starting point you personalize before sending.
💡 Pro tip

Add key facts such as dates, names and amounts in your description, then double-check the draft before sending, because AI can invent details.

Frequently asked questions

Should I send the draft as is?

No. Review it, add specific details and make sure everything is accurate before sending.

What tones are available?

You can choose from tones such as professional, friendly and formal.

Is my information stored?

No. It is used only to generate the draft and is not stored by us.
