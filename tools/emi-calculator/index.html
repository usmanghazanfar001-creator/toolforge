About the EMI / Loan Calculator

EMI (equated monthly installment) is the fixed amount you pay each month to repay a loan with interest. This calculator takes the loan amount, annual interest rate and tenure, then shows your monthly EMI, the total interest you will pay and the total repayment. It uses the standard reducing-balance formula and shows the working, so you can compare lenders, test different tenures and understand what a loan really costs before signing.

When to use it

  • Comparing loan offers from different banks with different rates.
  • Seeing how a longer tenure lowers the EMI but raises total interest.
  • Deciding how much you can afford to borrow for a car or home.
💡 Pro tip

Shortening the tenure by even a year can save a large amount of interest. Compare total interest, not just the monthly figure.

Frequently asked questions

What is the EMI formula?

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1), where P is the principal, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of monthly payments.

Does the result include processing fees?

No. It covers principal and interest only. Fees, insurance and taxes charged by the lender are extra.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is an estimate for planning. Confirm exact figures with your lender.
