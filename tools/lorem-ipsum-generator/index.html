About the Lorem Ipsum Generator

Designers and developers use placeholder text so a layout can be judged without distracting real copy. This generator produces Lorem Ipsum by the paragraph, sentence or word, so you can fill a mockup, test typography or check how a design handles long and short content. The text is generated instantly in your browser.

When to use it

  • Filling wireframes and website mockups with realistic text blocks.
  • Testing how a layout behaves with long headlines and paragraphs.
  • Creating dummy content for templates and themes.
💡 Pro tip

Replace placeholder text with real copy before launch. Pages shipped with Lorem Ipsum look unfinished and can hurt trust and SEO.

Frequently asked questions

What does Lorem Ipsum mean?

It is scrambled Latin derived from a work by Cicero. The words have no meaning, which is the point: readers focus on the layout, not the message.

Is it free to use?

Yes. The passage is in the public domain and free for any project.

Can I choose how much text to generate?

Yes. Select paragraphs, sentences or words and set the amount.
